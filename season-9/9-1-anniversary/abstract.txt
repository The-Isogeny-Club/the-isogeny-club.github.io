<p>The problem of finding isogenies between supersingular elliptic curves is the core hard problem of isogeny-based cryptography. Its study can be traced back to Kohel’s thesis in 1996, and isogeny-based cryptography has benefited from the remarkable stability of its complexity. We present this problem and its history, up to a recent algorithm reducing the asymptotic time complexity exponent from 1/2 to 1/3.</p>
